## Consistent design system

- Canvas: 1000 × 1500 px, portrait 2:3; export high-quality JPG or PNG.
- Brand treatment: Terrabloom Smart Finds wordmark, clean natural palette, high-contrast text and generous margins.
- Keep the actual product packaging visible and unaltered. Never generate substitute packaging, logos, certifications, ratings or review counts.
- Keep each Pin focused on one product and one clear action.
- Put the affiliate disclosure in the description and as readable on-image footer text.
- Use only the exact DCM tracking URL associated with that product after the redirect has been independently verified.
- Before scheduling, recheck listing stock, image accuracy, destination URL, audience market and disclosure.
