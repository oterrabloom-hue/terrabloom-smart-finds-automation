Privacy Policy

Terrabloom Smart Finds & T. Terrabloom Organic

Effective date: 17 November 2026  •  Last updated: 17 November 2026

1. Who we are

Terrabloom Smart Finds is a product-discovery brand sharing shopping inspiration for Pakistan and the USA. T. Terrabloom Organic is an organic-farming brand sharing farming tips and videos for Pakistani farmers. Both are run by the same team and share this website and privacy policy. You can reach us through our Facebook Pages (T.TerraBloom Organic and Terrabloom Smart Finds) or our YouTube channel (T.TerraBloom Organic).

2. Information we collect

We do not run accounts, sign-ups or checkouts on this website, and we do not ask you for your name, email address, phone number or payment details here.

When you visit this website, standard technical information may be collected automatically by the hosting provider (Netlify) and by your browser, such as your IP address, browser type, device type, pages visited and the time of your visit. This is used only to keep the site working and to understand overall traffic.

If you contact us through Facebook, Pinterest, YouTube, TikTok, Rumble or Dailymotion, those platforms handle your information under their own privacy policies, and we only see what you choose to share with us there (for example, a comment or a message).

3. How we use information

  • To run and improve this website and our content.
  • To respond to comments and messages you send us on our social pages.
  • To understand which topics and products our audience finds useful.

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information to anyone.

4. Affiliate links

Some links on our pages and in our video descriptions are affiliate links (for example, Daraz links). If you click one and buy something, we may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. These links are provided by our affiliate partner (DCMnetwork / Daraz). When you click an affiliate link you leave our pages and the retailer's own privacy policy applies.

5. Cookies and third-party services

This website itself does not set advertising or tracking cookies. The platforms we link to (YouTube, TikTok, Facebook, Pinterest, Rumble, Dailymotion, Daraz) may set their own cookies when you visit them, under their own policies. Your browser settings let you block or delete cookies at any time.

6. Children

Our content is made for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has shared personal information with us, please contact us through our Facebook Page and we will remove it.

7. Your choices

  • You can browse this website without giving us any personal information.
  • You can unfollow, unsubscribe or block our pages on any platform at any time.
  • You can ask us to delete a comment or message you sent us, by messaging the same Page or channel.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do, and the new version will apply from that date.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach us through the Terrabloom Smart Finds Facebook Page or the T.TerraBloom Organic Facebook Page.
